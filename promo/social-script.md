# Typing Aquarium — social scripts

Everything below is written to be recorded against screens that actually exist
in the app. Shot notes are in brackets so you can match voiceover to capture.

---

## MAIN SCRIPT — ~50 seconds

**0:00 — Hook**
> You type about 5,000 characters a day.
> And you get *absolutely nothing* for it.

*[Cold open on a normal desktop. Nothing special. Let it sit for a beat.]*

**0:05 — The turn**
> So I fixed that.

*[Tank window fades in over the desktop, fish already drifting.]*

**0:08 — The premise**
> This is Typing Aquarium. It sits on your desktop, counts every key you press,
> and every so often — a fish shows up.

*[Type a sentence on screen. Counter ticks. Splash sound. New fish swims in.]*

**0:17 — The hook that makes it a game**
> But here's the thing. You don't just *earn* fish.
> You have to earn each one **differently**.

*[Cut to Collection. Scroll it.]*

**0:24 — The conditions**
> The sardine wants 300 characters. Easy.
> The swordfish wants you to actually hit 85 words a minute.
> The coelacanth wants twelve unbroken minutes of focus.
> And the megamouth shark? Only shows up if you're typing before 8am.

*[Tap through three or four detail cards as you say them.]*

**0:36 — The tease**
> Thirty species. Everything you haven't caught is a silhouette.
> You can see the shape. You just don't know what it is yet.

*[Locked fish detail — the ??? and the shadow. Hold on it.]*

**0:42 — Stats**
> It's quietly keeping score the whole time.
> Your speed, your focus streaks, your best day.

*[Statistics page. Let the weekly bars land.]*

**0:47 — The vibe**
> And when you want to just… look at it —

*[Green traffic light → full screen tank. Ambient music up.]*

**0:52 — Close**
> Free. Mac only. Link below.
> Go get your fish.

## THINGS NOT TO SAY

- Don't call it "App Store" — it isn't, it's a direct download.
- Don't say "works on any Mac" — it's Apple Silicon only right now.
- Don't imply it reads what you type. It counts keystrokes, not content, and
  that distinction is the whole reason people will trust it with Accessibility
  permission. Say it plainly instead of skipping it.
